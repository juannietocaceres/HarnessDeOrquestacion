The idea, and DengueIA

DengueIA proved in Cali that forecasting dengue works; AedesAlert makes it open and replicable for the rest of the Valle.

DengueIA

Cali's health secretariat

  • 174 zones of 1 km²
  • ~2.2 million people
  • 93% reported effectiveness
  • Alerts up to 3 weeks ahead

AedesAlert

This project

  • Risk by comuna (city district)
  • Open data, open-source code
  • Prototype with Cali's 22 comunas
  • Then the other 8 very-high-risk municipalities

A complement, not a competitor

Say "reported effectiveness": the 93% is DengueIA's number, not ours. We do not claim to be more accurate; we make it open and replicable.

  • Complements DengueIA; does not compete

  • Benchmark: COP 12.9–16.7 million per contract (Cali, 2023)
  • Project funding: SGR-CTeI

Why not the PIC? That public health budget goes to the local public hospital (Palmira 2026: COP 2,300 million).

Precedent: the AEDES program against dengue, funded with royalties: COP 14,770 million.

The code is free; we charge for setup, training and support. The price is an assumption, to be validated. The PIC money goes to the local hospital, not to us.
